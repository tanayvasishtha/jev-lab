# Bundle bias

Jev answers every question in a call in one shared pass. Does bundling a question with 9 unrelated ones change its answer? 2,000 questions, asked alone and in 3 independent bundled compositions.

**Finding:** a real but small effect, 3.6% flip rate (95% CI 3.2%–4.1%), flat across position and composition. See [index.html](index.html) for the chart and full method.
